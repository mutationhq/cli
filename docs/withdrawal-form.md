If you are a consumer in the European Union, the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, you may cancel a Runner License purchase within 14 days after the contract is concluded, without giving a reason (section 11.5 of the [Mutation CLI License](../LICENSE-BINARY)). You do not have to use this form: an email to support@mutation.sh, or the withdrawal function ("Withdraw from contract here") on the website where you bought the Runner License, does the same. You may also withdraw by any other unequivocal statement sent to Mutation. Sending it before the withdrawal period expires is enough, even if Mutation receives it later. No particular form or email subject is required. Mutation confirms each cancellation by email and refunds every payment within 14 days, to the original payment method, without a fee.
