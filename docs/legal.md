## Before selling Runner Licenses

The pricing page at https://mutation.sh/pricing and its checkout must do each of these before the first sale (`LICENSE-BINARY` 3.6 to 3.8 and 11.5):

- **Seller identity:** the checkout, the order confirmation and every invoice name the seller as `LICENSE-BINARY` 1.1 does.
- **The Order:** before payment, the page shows everything section 3.6 lists: seller and customer, covered organization and affiliates, scope and quantity and how quantity is counted, covered versions, start and term, price, currency and payment schedule, taxes, renewal, cancellation and refund terms, support and Telemetry entitlements, and the incorporated terms.
- **Total with taxes:** Stripe Tax computes the sales tax or VAT for the customer's location, and the page shows the total with taxes before payment; consumer prices include them.
- **Acceptance:** an unchecked box the customer ticks: "I agree to the Mutation CLI License version 1.3 and the Order Terms displayed here. I am authorized to purchase for the named customer." The version named is the one in force. Mutation keeps a record of each acceptance.
- **Renewal consent:** beside the box, the renewal amount, its frequency (monthly or annually) and how to cancel, stated plainly, and the customer's express consent to them.
- **Payment button:** labelled "Buy Runner License: obligation to pay".
- **Early supply:** a consumer in the EU, the EEA or the UK who wants the Runner License to start within the 14 days asks for it by a separate, unticked box; without it, paid supply starts after those 14 days (section 11.5).
- **Confirmation:** an email with the accepted Order, the licence version and the withdrawal form ([model withdrawal form](withdrawal-form.md)), a durable medium.
- **Renewal reminders:** an email before each annual renewal, and before any renewal at a changed price, at least 15 and at most 45 days before it, stating the date, the amount and how to cancel. For consumers in California, also an annual reminder for monthly terms, and a notice of any fee change with cancellation instructions 7 to 30 days before it takes effect (section 3.8).
- **Online cancellation:** cancelling the renewal on the website takes no more steps than the purchase, and takes effect at the end of the paid period.
- **Withdrawal function:** for consumers in the EU, the EEA and the UK, a function labelled "Withdraw from contract here", followed by "Confirm withdrawal", available throughout the 14 days, and an email acknowledging each withdrawal; refunds within 14 days, to the original payment method.

## Decided

- **Governing law and courts:** Delaware law and the state and federal courts in Delaware (`LICENSE-BINARY` 17.1), with the consumer protections of 11.2 and 17.3.
- **No acceptance step at first run.** The CLI does not ask the user to accept the licence; acceptance rests on installing or using the Software after the licence or a notice of it was made available (`LICENSE-BINARY` 2.3). The notice is shown in the npm packages' READMEs, the installation guide, the README above the install commands and the Homebrew tap. Both reviewers rated this acceptance weaker in the United States and for EU consumers than an explicit step; the owner accepts that risk. Section 2.2 stays, so a later version that asks for acceptance needs no new licence text.
- **No switch that turns off every network feature.** Each feature uses the network only when its own work needs it, and its documentation says so; Telemetry has its own controls (`docs/privacy.md`). The EU reviewer recommended one switch under the Cyber Resilience Act's Annex I; the owner decided against it, and each network feature's security is assessed with the feature.
- **Support period:** the free CLI promises none (`LICENSE-BINARY` 13.1, `SECURITY.md`), beyond what the law requires; a paid agreement sets support and updates for its paid term (13.2). The EU reviewer advised a stated period of at least five years under the Cyber Resilience Act once its obligations apply (11 December 2027); that period must be stated before then, and 13.1 keeps what the law requires.
- **Telemetry:** always on in the free Software, as a condition of its licence (`LICENSE-BINARY` 7.4); opt-out under a paid agreement: a Runner License, during its term and for the uses its Order covers, is one. Nothing on the never-sent list in `docs/privacy.md` is ever sent. The EU reviewer advised opt-in in the EU, the EEA and the UK under Article 5(3) of the ePrivacy Directive and PECR; consent given by use after a notice is weaker there than an explicit act, and the owner accepts that risk.
- **Runner License:** `mutation` is free for personal and commercial use in a person's workstation development session, including agents and git hooks within that session. Pipeline Use is free for Public Repositories and Personal Non-Commercial Projects. Other Pipeline Use requires a paid Runner License from https://mutation.sh/pricing after one 15-day evaluation per organization or independent individual. Hosted and self-hosted workflows are treated alike. Licensing is a legal requirement; the Software performs no license check. See sections 1.14–1.17 and 3.4 of `LICENSE-BINARY` for the definitions and evaluation rules. For a Business User, the liability cap is the greater of USD 50 and Runner License fees paid in the twelve months before the event giving rise to the first claim (10.2), and paid obligations are not excluded by 8.3 or 10.2(A) and (B) (10.6).
- **Seller:** Mutation.sh itself sells Runner Licenses and is the seller of record; Stripe processes the payments and is not a reseller or merchant of record. Mutation.sh, reached at support@mutation.sh (`LICENSE-BINARY` 1.1, `docs/privacy.md`, `docs/withdrawal-form.md`). The panel advised naming a legal form, a geographical address, a registration, a VAT number and a telephone number, which EU and UK consumer law requires of a trader that sells to consumers; there is no legal entity yet, and the owner accepts that risk. They are added to these documents and to the checkout once an entity exists. Order records are kept in Stripe for as long as Stripe retains them; Stripe's privacy policy is https://stripe.com/privacy.
- **Taxes:** Stripe Tax computes and collects the sales tax and VAT of each Order; the checkout shows the total with taxes (`LICENSE-BINARY` 3.7).
- **Renewal:** Runner Licenses renew automatically, monthly or annually as the Order states, with express consent at checkout, a reminder 15 to 45 days before each annual renewal and before a renewal at a changed price, and online cancellation that takes effect at the end of the paid period. Evaluation never renews into a purchase (`LICENSE-BINARY` 3.8).
- **Consumers:** Runner Licenses are sold to businesses and consumers. Consumers in the EU, the EEA and the UK may withdraw within 14 days, by email, the withdrawal function or the [model withdrawal form](withdrawal-form.md) (`LICENSE-BINARY` 11.5).
- **Support mailbox:** support@mutation.sh runs on Zoho Mail in Zoho's EU data centres (`docs/privacy.md`).
