## Decided

- **Governing law and courts:** Delaware law and the state and federal courts in Delaware (`LICENSE-BINARY` 17.1), with the consumer protections of 11.2 and 17.3.
- **No acceptance step at first run.** The CLI does not ask the user to accept the licence; acceptance rests on installing or using the Software after the licence or a notice of it was made available (`LICENSE-BINARY` 2.3). The notice is shown in the npm packages' READMEs, the installation guide, the README above the install commands and the Homebrew tap. Both reviewers rated this acceptance weaker in the United States and for EU consumers than an explicit step; the owner accepts that risk. Section 2.2 stays, so a later version that asks for acceptance needs no new licence text.
- **No switch that turns off every network feature.** Each feature uses the network only when its own work needs it, and its documentation says so; Telemetry has its own controls (`docs/privacy.md`). The EU reviewer recommended one switch under the Cyber Resilience Act's Annex I; the owner decided against it, and each network feature's security is assessed with the feature.
- **Support period:** the free CLI promises none (`LICENSE-BINARY` 13.1, `SECURITY.md`), beyond what the law requires; a paid agreement sets support and updates for its paid term (13.2). The EU reviewer advised a stated period of at least five years under the Cyber Resilience Act once its obligations apply (11 December 2027); that period must be stated before then, and 13.1 keeps what the law requires.
- **Telemetry:** always on in the free Software, as a condition of its licence (`LICENSE-BINARY` 7.4); opt-out under a paid agreement. Nothing on the never-sent list in `docs/privacy.md` is ever sent. The EU reviewer advised opt-in in the EU, the EEA and the UK under Article 5(3) of the ePrivacy Directive and PECR; consent given by use after a notice is weaker there than an explicit act, and the owner accepts that risk.
- **Support mailbox:** support@mutation.sh runs on Zoho Mail in Zoho's EU data centres (`docs/privacy.md`).
